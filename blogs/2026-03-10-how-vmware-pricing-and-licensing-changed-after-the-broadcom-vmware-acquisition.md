Dell, a major VMware stakeholder, agreed to sell VMware to Broadcom when the latter officially announced its plans for the takeover in May 2022. It took almost 18 months and $61 billion to strike the deal. The acquisition process was followed by scrutiny from investigations by the EU, US, UK, and China. Besides, controversy arose as many loyal VMware customers feared that they would have to look elsewhere because of Broadcom’s track record.
